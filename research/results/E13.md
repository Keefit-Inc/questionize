# E13 — Real-photo capture, explanation handling and uncertainty

Three deliberately selected photo scenarios share two known difficult source questions: a completion alone, that page with its printed key/explanation, and a boldface-role question alone. These are private local Meta calls using the same capture, blind-solving and diagnosis functions as Snap, not learner traffic or a new independent benchmark. No source material or question was imported into an account or Bank.

| Version | Source/diagnosis delivered | Text roles matched | Detailed explanation delivered | Execution errors |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| v1 (`20261030T230404Z-e13-photos`) | 1/3 | 1/3 | Not separately recorded | 2 |
| v2 (`20261030T231016Z-e13-photos`) | 3/3 | 3/3 | 0/3 | 0 |
| v3 (`20261030T231836Z-e13-photos`) | 3/3 | 3/3 | 1/3 | 0 |

Each version attempted twelve logical model calls without retries. A separate analyst diagnostic timed out after 90 seconds. V1 exposed an invalid fallback schema after unsuccessful analyst calls; V2 fixed source delivery but all analyses remained unavailable. V3 requires explicit fields in the analysis-only response instead of a wholly optional schema. Model outputs cannot replace the already captured source fields. No capture/solve/analysis timeout was increased, and the images, source keys and crops were unchanged. Provider variability and load were not controlled, so this is an operational repair observation, not a precise causal latency estimate.

In v3, **all three analyst calls returned structured analysis**. Two cases had conflicting independent answers and therefore withheld a definitive key, explanation and learner misconception; the printed-reference case delivered its checked analysis. Thus one detailed explanation plus two deliberate uncertainty holds is different from three successful explanations. All three evidence-handling contracts passed. Two of the three main answers matched the source key, but the scenarios share parents and were selected after errors: this is not a population accuracy measure.

The no-reference case in v2 also demonstrated the original concern: both AI calls agreed on an answer that disagreed with the printed reference, which was withheld in that scenario. The result stayed `ai_estimated`; it did not become a gold label or a definite learner error. AI agreement is insufficient ground truth.

When detailed analysis is absent or inconsistent with the selected answer, the service preserves the captured question and reference, withholds unsupported option judgments, and does not generate practice from a missing design. The learner screen explains the deferral rather than showing a provisional difficulty/diagnosis as established. Source/reference originals remain local; only abstract confidence/version observations are eligible for server research storage.

These checks support a **small operator pilot with verifiable reference material**. They do not establish human explanation correctness, robust camera/OCR performance, 99.9% solving, general item quality or learner benefit. Earlier failures remain in the append-only ledger; no favorable rerun replaces them.

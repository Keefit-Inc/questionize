# E16 — Local 1B feasibility; candidate held

The local Meta checkpoint runs and an actual small adapter can be trained on the
development machine without paid inference. **Useful reasoning improvement is not
demonstrated.** Neither the procedure nor the adapter is adopted. Production is
unchanged. These are development pilots, not the unfinished E15 confirmation.

## Existing OG development pilot

Two items per subtype, selected deterministically before inference: 16 previously
exposed items from the existing OG development cohort. Keys and explanations were
excluded from model inputs; every attempt counts. The model is the community MLX
4-bit conversion of Meta Llama 3.2 1B Instruct, revision
`08231374eeacb049a0eade7922910865b8fce912`.

| Condition | Key matches | Missing final label | Output tokens | Median seconds/item |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Base, brief explanation then answer, 256-token limit | 5/16 | 0 | 2,382 | 0.6444 |
| Same base plus fixed procedure, same limit | 3/16 | 3 | 3,490 | 0.9929 |
| Base, one-token A–E constrained choice | 0/16 | 0 | 16 | 0.1032 |
| Same constrained choice with original-logic adapter | 5/16 | 0 | 16 | 0.1139 |

The adapter does not beat the retained generative baseline. It answers E on 14
items and B on two; always answering E would also obtain 5/16. This posthoc constant
control is a diagnostic, not a deployable comparison selected without labels.
The apparent increase over the poor constrained baseline does not establish
useful CR transfer. Source-key matches are not human explanation-quality ratings.

## Original logic training feasibility

Original, programmatic exercises use satisfiable Boolean premises and one uniquely
entailed option. Exhaustive enumeration supplies the reference; a separately
implemented rule derivation and offline interpreter tests check it. No book text,
expert explanation, paid teacher or learner response was used in training.

Training has 128 instances of only four logical structures. Development has 24
instances of three different structures. Sixteen instances of two other structures
remain unused for confirmation. Related structure families and template variants
are not independent natural-language CR problems.

An initial generative probe scored 0/24 with 16 missing final answers. Before
training, a format-neutral choice control and matching label-only training targets
were registered separately. The original generation outputs remain preserved.

| Matched development choice condition | Correct | Answer distribution |
|---|---:|---|
| Base | 2/24 | A: 24 |
| Final adapter | 4/24 | E: 16; B: 8 |

Always answering C would score 8/24 on this cohort. The trained model remains
dominated by label bias and fails this diagnostic. No statistically independent
structure-level improvement or general GMAT mastery is established.

The fixed run used 80 iterations, batch two, four adapted layers, rank eight,
learning rate 1e-4 and seed 41. It completed in 38.657 seconds. Training loss and
validation loss fell, but that is not a task-quality result. Training peak memory
and energy were not instrumented. Evaluation allocator peaks were approximately
0.96–1.49 GB, excluding system/process overhead; this is not total device memory.

## Decision and next evidence

Hold this candidate. A practical follow-up must improve decision quality and
label-position robustness rather than only teach answer formatting. Check data
coverage, longer supported reasoning supervision and independent verifiable
procedures on small development groups before broader evaluation. Testing a
larger affordable model would be a capacity comparison, not our learning gain.
There is no further paid batch scheduled by this experiment.

The local inference/training cost in API fees was zero; hardware, power and setup
are not free. Original sources, full per-item outputs, model files and the adapter
remain local. Aggregate measurements and file hashes are in [E16.json](E16.json).
The ledger records both pilots as development scope. No 100% solving, learner
diagnosis, explanation quality, writing quality or live service improvement is
claimed.
